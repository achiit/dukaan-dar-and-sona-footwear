About the shop

Two years ago I opened this shop in Balsoo with one simple idea: a man from Kulgam should not have to travel far to dress well. I started small, with a few racks near Chinar Chowk, and I still choose the stock myself.

Most of my customers are neighbours, friends and their families. They come in for one shirt and leave with a jacket, because we sit and talk about what suits them. That is how a shop becomes part of the neighbourhood.

Wear our clothes and build your aura. It is not just our tagline. It is what I want every customer to feel when he walks out of the door.
